1.1.2.0Nov 17, 2025dispersion / aim assist
  • Aim Assist fully reset to Open Beta tuning after players found the launch version's increased long-range slowdown made high-zoom aiming feel less smooth.
  • Dispersion globally reduced slightly across all weapon types; fixed an unintended dispersion-increase rate.
  • Non-Recon-class sniper rifle accuracy improved.
  • Increased the weighting of long-range performance in automatic-weapon balance, benefiting PW7A2 and KV9 specifically.
  • Reduced recoil and recoil variation on LMR27, M39, and SVDM for better long-range reliability.
Everything else in 1.1.2.0: bug fixes, UI, cosmetics, audio, Portal, settings, AI, REDSEC misc
  • Player & movement: Controller input and deadzone fixes, new landing and death animations, vaulting/ladder/takedown polish, fixed several third-person desync issues.
  • Maps & modes: Added the Sabotage event mode and Eastwood; fixed black-screen spawns, late-join matchmaking issues, and Strikepoint destruction-reset bugs.
  • UI & HUD: Improved downed-state kill-log visibility, added a detailed Strikepoint player-count display, fixed several icon bugs.
  • Settings: Added an auto-sprint option and an instant-knife-swap keybind.
  • Single Player: Fixed AI squadmate command responsiveness, a shader stutter, and several audio/subtitle sync bugs.
  • Portal: Added new music-control scripting functions, fixed terrain raycasting.
  • Audio: New ping sounds, foley/turret/door audio fixes, multiple voice-over line corrections.
  • Hardware: Fixed a bug capping framerate at 300fps on Nvidia cards.
Source: Battlefield 6 Game Update 1.1.2.0, posted Nov 17, 2025
1.1.1.0Oct 24, 2025dispersion rework
  • Dispersion rebalanced by damage profile: low-damage weapons get slightly more dispersion growth when firing; high-damage weapons get slightly less.
  • Sniper rifles used by non-Recon classes no longer get increased weapon spread as a penalty; instead, weapon sway now begins during the zoom transition, preserving Recon's precision edge a different way.
Everything else in 1.1.1.0: bug fixes, UI, cosmetics, audio, Portal, settings, AI, REDSEC misc
  • Player & movement: Smoother landings and stance transitions, steadier aim/hitbox alignment, animation polish across takedowns, ladders, and mounting.
  • Maps & modes: Fixed fog/smoke lingering too long after destruction, improved indoor/outdoor exposure transitions, several per-map fixes (Mirak Valley, Siege of Cairo, Liberation Peak, New Sobek City, Manhattan Bridge).
  • UI & HUD: New unlock markers for weapons/gadgets, gadget preview videos added, fixed HUD display bugs at extreme aspect ratios.
  • Settings: Added Flick Look bindings for vehicles, fixed aspect-ratio display issues.
  • Single Player: Fixed a lean-out bug in Mission 01 and a black-screen bug in the Night Raid mission.
  • Portal: Fixed the host-experience preview image and a server-join crash.
  • Audio: Large overhaul pass: new projectile flyby and impact sounds, haptics tuning, fixed missing or incorrect ambient and reload audio across many maps and weapons.
Source: Battlefield 6 Update 1.1.1.0, posted Oct 24, 2025
1.0.1.0Oct 9, 2025launch baseline
  • General recoil adjustments across multiple weapons at launch.
  • ADS accuracy made more stable while moving, but less accurate when firing mid-jump; increased bullet spread firing automatic weapons while ADS.
  • Bolt-action rifles: the first-shot accuracy boost now applies to every shot fired, not just the first.
  • M87A1 shotgun requires more pellets to secure a kill.
  • Increased jump-repeat penalty and slightly lowered jump height; increased weapon inaccuracy while jumping or sliding; reduced slide-to-jump momentum.
Everything else in 1.0.1.0: bug fixes, UI, cosmetics, audio, Portal, settings, AI, REDSEC misc
  • Player & movement: Reduced landing/vault jank, smoother hipfire-to-ADS transition, fixed weapon-dispersion spikes, faster drag-and-revive cancel, updated parachute and swim animations.
  • Maps & modes: Revised Rush/Breakthrough layouts from Open Beta feedback, closed spawn-killing and out-of-bounds exploits on Siege of Cairo and Iberian Offensive, improved Operation Firestorm traversal, lighting, and performance.
  • UI & HUD: Clearer loadout lock reasons, fixed class/skin name display bugs, added an XP-loss warning on early match exit, reworked class-identity UI, improved pings and nametags.
  • Settings: Split PC and console settings, added a VO volume slider and several motion-sickness accessibility options (roll-free combat roll, reduced sprint camera bob), added a minimap size option.
  • Portal: Removed crossplay from server hosting, fixed AI soldiers ignoring loadout restriction rules.
  • Audio: Game-wide audio overhaul: clearer pings, refined weapon and vehicle mixes, new suppression effects, tuned shotgun and SMG sound profiles.
  • Network: Netcode tuning aimed at reducing fast time-to-death, damage-without-a-visible-hit, and dying-behind-cover cases.
